Aviation aftermarket Florida, US 2026 — ongoing

Core exchange economics in Business Central

When an aviation parts distributor sells a rotable component, the customer pays for the part and a refundable core deposit, returned when the worn unit comes back for overhaul. Business Central models none of it.

I designed companion document structures that carry the core value separately from the part, segregated credit management so core exposure doesn't consume a customer's trading limit, automated ledger application to net core credits at operational volume, and purchase order lifecycle logic that waits on the physical return rather than the invoice.

  • AL extensions
  • Application engine
  • Posting groups
  • Master data APIs
Direct-to-consumer retail US · UK · EU 2026

Custom commerce integration for a multi-brand retailer

The standard Shopify connector couldn't carry this retailer's reality: two brands, three warehouse regions, payment service provider settlement, zero-value bundle orders, and refunds that split into two different accounting paths depending on whether goods came back.

I built the integration from the Business Central side — published API endpoints, staging tables with replay protection, job queue orchestration, OAuth 2.0 against Entra ID, and settlement reconciliation against a virtual bank account.
